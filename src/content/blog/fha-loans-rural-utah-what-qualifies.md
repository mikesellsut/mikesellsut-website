FHA loans work well across Central Utah, particularly for buyers who don't qualify for USDA's income or property-location limits but still want a lower down payment than conventional financing requires. Here's what actually qualifies.

## Down Payment and Credit

FHA loans allow down payments as low as 3.5% for qualifying buyers, with more flexible credit requirements than conventional financing. This makes FHA a strong option for first-time buyers in Richfield, Ephraim, Manti, Nephi, or any of the more populated towns in my territory where USDA's rural-area boundaries may not apply.

## Property Requirements

FHA loans require the property to meet specific minimum safety and livability standards, verified through an FHA-specific appraisal:

- The home must be safe, sound, and secure — a fixer-upper with major structural, electrical, or roofing issues may not qualify without repairs first
- The property must be the buyer's primary residence
- Rural properties with well and septic systems can qualify, but the well and septic must meet specific distance and safety standards, which the FHA appraisal will check

## Manufactured Homes and FHA

FHA loans can finance manufactured homes, provided the home is permanently affixed to a foundation on land the buyer owns, meets HUD construction standards, and passes the required inspection. This is a common path for manufactured home buyers in Gunnison, Moroni, Delta, and Aurora, where manufactured housing is common.

## FHA vs. USDA: Which Fits Your Situation

- **Choose USDA** if the property is in a qualifying rural area and your household income falls under the limit — it offers 0% down, which FHA doesn't match.
- **Choose FHA** if the property is in a more populated area that doesn't qualify for USDA, or if your income exceeds USDA's limits, since FHA has no income cap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the minimum down payment for an FHA loan?
As low as 3.5% for qualifying buyers, based on credit score and other underwriting factors.

### Can I get an FHA loan on a home with a well and septic system?
Yes, but the well and septic must meet FHA's specific safety and distance standards, verified during the FHA appraisal. Not every rural well/septic setup will automatically pass.

### Does FHA have income limits like USDA?
No. FHA loans have no income limit, unlike USDA loans, which makes FHA a better fit for buyers who exceed USDA's income thresholds.

### Can I finance a manufactured home with an FHA loan in Central Utah?
Yes, provided the home is permanently affixed to a foundation on owned land and meets HUD construction standards.
