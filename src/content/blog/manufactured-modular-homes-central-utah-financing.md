Manufactured and modular homes are a realistic, financeable option across Central Utah — common in Gunnison, Moroni, Delta, Aurora, and Salina in particular — but financing depends on a few specifics that differ from a typical site-built home purchase.

## Manufactured vs. Modular: Know the Difference

- **Manufactured homes** are built entirely in a factory to federal HUD code and transported to the site as one or more sections.
- **Modular homes** are also factory-built but constructed to the same state and local building codes as site-built homes, then assembled on-site on a permanent foundation.

This distinction matters for financing and appraisal — modular homes are generally treated more like traditional site-built homes, while manufactured homes have additional requirements.

## The Foundation Rule

For nearly any financing type — conventional, FHA, or USDA — a manufactured home must be permanently affixed to a foundation on land the buyer owns (not leased) to qualify for standard mortgage financing. A manufactured home on a rented lot, or not permanently attached to a foundation, typically requires personal property (chattel) financing instead, which carries different terms and higher rates.

## Financing Options

- **FHA loans** can finance manufactured homes that meet HUD standards, are on a permanent foundation, and pass FHA's specific inspection requirements.
- **USDA loans** can also finance manufactured homes in qualifying rural areas, subject to USDA's own construction and installation standards.
- **Conventional loans** are available but often come with more conservative terms for manufactured homes compared to site-built homes.

## What to Verify Before Buying

- The home's age and HUD certification label (a metal tag, usually near the electrical panel) confirming it meets federal manufactured housing standards
- Whether the home is currently, and was originally, installed on a permanent foundation
- Title status — manufactured homes can carry a personal property title separate from the land, which needs to be converted to real property status for standard mortgage financing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a mortgage on a manufactured home in Utah?
Yes, through FHA, USDA (in qualifying areas), or conventional financing, provided the home is permanently affixed to a foundation on land you own and meets the relevant program's construction standards.

### What's the difference between manufactured and modular homes for financing?
Modular homes are built to the same codes as site-built homes and are generally financed similarly to traditional homes. Manufactured homes are built to federal HUD code and have additional requirements, including the foundation and title-conversion rules.

### Can I finance a manufactured home on rented land?
Generally no, not through standard mortgage financing. A manufactured home on leased land typically requires personal property (chattel) financing instead, which carries different terms.

### Where are manufactured homes most common in Central Utah?
Gunnison, Moroni, Delta, Aurora, and Salina all have a meaningful number of manufactured homes for sale, generally at more affordable price points than site-built alternatives.
