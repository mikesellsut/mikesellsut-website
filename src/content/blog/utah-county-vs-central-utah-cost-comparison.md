If you're priced out of Utah County, the question isn't whether Central Utah is cheaper — it is, consistently and significantly — it's whether the tradeoffs actually fit your life. Here's an honest comparison.

## Housing Costs

Utah County (Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork) has seen home prices climb well beyond what many first-time and move-up buyers can manage, especially for anything with land. Central Utah — particularly Juab County, the closest of my four counties to Utah County — consistently offers meaningfully more home and land for the same budget, with that gap widening further in Sanpete, Sevier, and especially Millard County.

## Commute

This is the real tradeoff. From **Nephi (Juab County)**, Provo is about 45 minutes — genuinely workable for daily commuters. From **Sanpete, Sevier, or Millard**, you're looking at 90 minutes to two and a half hours, which only makes sense if you're remote, retired, or only need to be in Utah County occasionally.

## Lot Size and Land

Utah County lots have shrunk considerably as the market has densified — new construction increasingly means smaller lots and closer neighbors. In all four Central Utah counties, larger lots and acreage are the norm rather than the exception, and land purchases separate from a home are realistic and affordable, particularly in Millard and Sanpete Counties.

## Schools and Amenities

Utah County has more schools, more specialty medical care, more shopping, and more entertainment options — that's the honest tradeoff for the density and price. Richfield in Sevier County has the most comparable infrastructure among the Central Utah towns, but none of the four counties match Utah County's amenity density. What you gain instead is space, lower traffic, and a slower pace of life.

## Who This Move Makes Sense For

- Remote workers who don't need daily Utah County access
- Buyers who value land and space over proximity to retail and entertainment
- First-time buyers who feel priced out of Utah County entirely
- Retirees looking to stretch retirement savings further
- Commuters specifically willing to settle in Nephi for the 45-minute drive

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it cheaper to live in Central Utah than Utah County?
Yes, consistently. All four counties I serve — Sanpete, Sevier, Millard, and Juab — offer more home and land per dollar than Utah County, with Millard County offering the largest gap and Juab County (due to its commute advantage) the smallest.

### Can I still commute to Utah County from Central Utah?
Only realistically from Nephi in Juab County, about 45 minutes to Provo. The other three counties are a two to two-and-a-half-hour drive, workable for occasional trips but not a daily commute.

### What do I give up moving from Utah County to Central Utah?
Primarily amenity density — restaurants, retail, specialty healthcare, and entertainment options. What you gain is significantly more space, lower cost of living, and a slower pace of life.
