If price-per-acre or price-per-square-foot is your main filter, Millard County is very likely your answer in Central Utah. It's the most affordable of the four counties I serve, and it has the most available land — but it also has the slowest inventory turnover, so patience matters more here than speed.

## Who Buys in Millard County

- **Rural lifestyle and land buyers** looking for acreage at the lowest price point in the region, particularly around Delta's agricultural land.
- **First-time buyers** priced out of larger markets who need every dollar to stretch further.
- **Wasatch Front commuters** — Fillmore is about two hours from Provo, which is a longer commute than Juab County but still workable for buyers who only go in a few days a week or work remotely most of the time.

## Financing in Millard County

- **USDA loans (0% down)** cover nearly all of Millard County, making it one of the strongest markets in Central Utah for buyers who want to buy with little or no money down.
- **FHA loans** apply well to in-town homes in Fillmore and Delta.
- **Conventional financing** is typical for larger acreage or higher-value properties where USDA's property and income limits don't fit.
- **Manufactured homes** are common in both Fillmore and Delta and generally price-competitive with site-built homes; the same foundation and land-ownership rules apply for financing.
- **Land loans** here often represent the best value in the entire territory — Delta's agricultural land in particular can be significantly cheaper per acre than comparable land in Sanpete or Juab. Expect fewer lenders to work in this space and a larger down payment than a typical home loan.

## Town-by-Town Notes

**Fillmore**, Utah's original territorial capital, has small-town character, the Territorial Statehouse State Park Museum, and views of the Pahvant Range. **Delta** is the agricultural heartbeat of the county — wide-open land, a stable farming economy, and proximity to Great Basin landscape and Meadow Hot Springs, a natural thermal pool that draws regional visitors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest county for land in Central Utah?
Millard County generally offers the lowest price per acre of the four counties I serve, particularly around Delta, though pricing varies significantly by parcel size, access, and water rights.

### Is Millard County a good commute option to Provo or Salt Lake City?
Fillmore is about two hours from Provo — workable for buyers who commute only occasionally or work remotely, but longer than Juab County's roughly 45-minute commute.

### Does USDA financing cover Millard County?
Nearly all of Millard County qualifies for USDA Rural Development loans, making it one of the strongest 0%-down markets in the region.

### Are there manufactured homes for sale in Millard County?
Yes, manufactured and modular homes are common in both Fillmore and Delta and are typically financeable when permanently affixed to a foundation on land you own.
