If you're being priced out of Utah County but still need to get there regularly, Juab County — specifically Nephi — is almost always the first place I point people. It's 45 minutes to Provo and just over an hour to Salt Lake City, which makes it the only one of my four counties that works as a true daily-commute option, and that access is exactly why it's the fastest-growing and most competitive market I cover.

## Who Buys in Juab County

- **Utah County commuters** priced out of Provo, Orem, or Lehi who need daily freeway access and can't go further out than Nephi.
- **Salt Lake County escapees** willing to trade a longer commute for significantly lower home prices and more space.
- **Rural lifestyle seekers** drawn to Mona and Levan for true small-town living with Mt. Nebo as a backdrop, at the cost of more limited inventory.

## Financing in Juab County

- **Conventional and FHA loans** are the most common financing in Nephi, where inventory and pricing look more like a typical suburban market than the rest of Central Utah.
- **USDA loans (0% down)** are generally available in Mona and Levan and in outlying parts of the county outside Nephi's more populated core — eligibility depends on the specific address, which I confirm before you write an offer.
- **New construction** is active in Nephi, with several subdivisions in progress; builders here often have preferred-lender relationships worth comparing against outside financing.
- **Manufactured homes and land** are more limited in Juab than in Millard or Sanpete, simply because less land is available this close to the Wasatch Front — when it comes up, it tends to move quickly.

## Town-by-Town Notes

**Nephi** sits right off I-15, dominated by Mt. Nebo — the tallest peak in the Wasatch Range at 11,928 feet — and the Nebo Loop Scenic Byway. Home prices here have risen significantly over the past several years but remain a fraction of Utah County pricing. **Mona** and **Levan** are smaller, quieter, and far more limited on inventory, but offer real rural living with mountain views and easy access to Yuba State Park for fishing and boating.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far is Nephi from Provo and Salt Lake City?
Nephi is about 45 minutes from Provo and just over an hour from Salt Lake City, making it the most commute-friendly of the four counties I serve.

### Is Juab County a competitive market to buy in?
Yes — Nephi in particular sees multiple-offer situations on well-priced homes more often than Sanpete, Sevier, or Millard. Being pre-approved before you start touring matters most here.

### Can I buy land in Juab County?
Land is more limited in Juab than in the other three counties simply due to proximity to the Wasatch Front, but it does come available, particularly around Mona and Levan.

### Does USDA financing work in Juab County?
USDA loans are generally available in Mona, Levan, and outlying areas, though Nephi's more populated core may not qualify depending on the specific address — I check this property-by-property.
